## Customer requirements distilled from the RFP
### Technical Scope
- Describe the target platform for the custom logistics and order-tracking solution.
- Name the systems to integrate with, including SAP S/4HANA and the carrier EDI gateway.
- Explain how order, inventory, and finance data will flow across the solution and integrations.
- State how the solution supports zero-data-loss migration.
- Cover testing scope across functional, integration, performance, and security testing.

### PM & Delivery
- Provide the implementation and deployment approach across requirements elaboration, design, build, deploy, UAT, training, and cutover.
- Show the timeline alignment to build/integration weeks 5–18, UAT weeks 19–22, and end-of-Q1 go-live.
- Identify customer dependencies and assumptions affecting schedule feasibility.
- Describe documentation, training, and knowledge-transfer deliverables.
- Explain support and maintenance scope including SLA-based support, patch cadence, helpdesk, and audits.

### Commercials
- Price the build as milestone-based fixed price.
- State that change requests are handled on a T&M basis.
- Provide separate pricing for 12 months of support.
- Align the commercial construct to the customer's evaluation criteria and milestone preference.

### Case Studies
- Provide credible case studies relevant to logistics and SAP-integrated delivery.
- Substantiate delivery credibility with measurable outcomes.
- Highlight examples that support the proposed implementation approach and timeline.
