## 1. Introduction

Aurora Healthcare Network Pvt. Ltd. ("Client") is a mid-market multi-specialty hospital group operating fourteen hospitals and twenty-two outpatient clinics across Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana. The Client is seeking software services from a qualified IT vendor ("Vendor") to design, build, deploy, and support a **custom Patient Scheduling & Care Coordination Platform** that will replace the Client's existing in-house system, which has reached end of life.

Vendors are invited to respond with a **Statement of Work (SOW)** covering scope, deliverables, timeline, roles and responsibilities, commercial model, and relevant credentials. This RFP is being issued to a shortlist of three vendors. The Client reserves the right not to award.

## 2. Background

The Client's current scheduling and care-coordination system was built in-house over the last decade. It does not integrate cleanly with the Client's recently deployed **Epic EHR** (electronic health record) platform, lacks modern HL7/FHIR connectivity to its lab and imaging partners, and provides limited real-time visibility to clinicians, front-desk staff, and downstream patients. Replacement is a board-approved priority for the current financial year, with a fixed go-live target of **end of Q1 of the next calendar year**.

## 3. Objectives

The Vendor's proposed solution must:

- Deliver a custom Patient Scheduling & Care Coordination Platform meeting the functional and non-functional requirements summarised in §4 (full requirements pack available in Annexure A on request).
- Integrate cleanly with the Client's **Epic EHR** (Patient, Appointment, Clinical Orders, Billing modules) via standard FHIR R4 APIs.
- Integrate with the Client's **partner-facing HL7/FHIR gateway** for lab order placement, lab result delivery, and imaging report exchange with external diagnostic chains.
- Migrate operational data from the legacy system with **zero data loss** and a documented reconciliation report.
- Provide a self-service portal for the Client's patients to book appointments, view reports, and download discharge documents.
- Provide twelve (12) months of post go-live support and maintenance, priced separately and renewable annually.

## 4. Scope of Work

### 4.1 Software Development
The Vendor will:
- Conduct a detailed requirements elaboration with the Client's clinical, operations, and IT stakeholders.
- Design, develop, and deploy the platform per the agreed specifications.
- Provide regular progress updates and engage in collaborative discussions throughout the development lifecycle.
- Conduct comprehensive functional, integration, performance, and security testing.
- Assist the Client in migrating and reconciling operational data from the existing system.
- Deliver the final solution with complete technical documentation, runbooks, and end-user training materials.

### 4.2 Implementation and Deployment
The Vendor will:
- Plan and execute a controlled cutover from the legacy system to the new platform.
- Conduct user acceptance testing (UAT) with the Client's nominated stakeholders.
- Provide role-based training to the Client's clinical, front-desk, and IT teams.
- Collaborate with the Client's IT team to ensure proper integration and configuration with the existing infrastructure (Epic EHR, HL7/FHIR gateway, identity provider, monitoring stack).

### 4.3 Support and Maintenance (Year 1)
The Vendor will:
- Address software issues, defects, and incidents per agreed SLAs (P1 — 1 business hour response / 4 hour resolution target; P2 — 4 hours / 1 business day; P3 — 1 business day / 5 business days).
- Release regular patches and minor enhancements.
- Conduct quarterly system health and security audits.
- Operate a dedicated helpdesk with a ticketing system accessible to the Client's IT team.
- Provide a knowledge-transfer programme to enable the Client's IT team to take over Level-1 support at the end of Year 1, at the Client's option.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

### 6.1 Vendor Responsibilities
The Vendor shall:
- Assign a named Project Manager and a dedicated delivery team (architects, developers, testers, integration specialists, support engineers).
- Conduct weekly project meetings and provide written progress reports.
- Adhere to the agreed project timeline, milestones, and quality criteria.
- Ensure the solution complies with industry best practices, the Client's security standards, and applicable regulations (incl. data residency in India and patient-data protection under the DPDP Act 2023).
- Maintain strict confidentiality and security of the Client's data and information.
- Provide all licences, tools, and infrastructure required to execute the project, other than items expressly listed as Client-provided.

### 6.2 Client Responsibilities
The Client shall:
- Appoint a Programme Manager as the single point of contact for the Vendor.
- Make clinical, business, and IT SMEs available for requirements, design reviews, UAT, and training.
- Provide timely access to Epic EHR, the HL7/FHIR gateway, identity systems, and the test/production environments as needed.
- Make decisions and sign off deliverables within the windows agreed in the SOW.
- Make milestone payments per the agreed payment schedule.

## 7. Commercial Model

- **Build** — milestone-based fixed price. Vendor to propose the milestone breakdown, deliverables tied to each milestone, and payment percentage per milestone (totalling 100% of build value).
- **Change requests** — time-and-materials at agreed rate-card rates; rate card to be included in the SOW.
- **Support & Maintenance — Year 1** — priced separately as an annual fee, with a one-year renewal option at agreed escalation.
- **Travel and out-of-pocket expenses** — at actuals against pre-approved limits.

All amounts to be quoted in **INR**, exclusive of applicable taxes.
